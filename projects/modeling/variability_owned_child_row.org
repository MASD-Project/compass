#+title: Profile: Owned child row
#+description: A row that belongs to a content-addressed row, keyed by its owner's key and its place among its siblings, written in bulk with its owner.

This page is a profile of the =masd.codegen.entity= models of compass. A
model binds it with =:profile: owned-child-row= in its =* Flags= drawer.

* Purpose

A heading, a section or a link of a document, or a step of a scenario: a
row that exists only as part of its owner, keyed by the owner's key and an
=ord=, its place in the owner's vector, and deleted with it. Both key
columns stay out of the row type; the COPY writer, which the profile
switches on, takes them beside the row.

* Reference entity

=compass.db.heading=, a heading of an org document.

* Assignments

The profile assigns no feature.
