#+title: compass.serve.testing_queries
#+description: Codegen operation model for the document service's reads of a sprint and its test campaigns: its stories and tasks with their states, what needs testing in an environment, a run with its step results and a campaign's notes.
#+type: masd.codegen.operation
#+component: serve
#+namespace: compass::serve::messages
#+entity_singular: testing_queries
#+brief: The document service's reads of a sprint, its testing and its campaigns.
#+filetags: :model:operation:serve:
#+created: 2026-11-23
#+updated: 2026-11-23

What a sprint holds, what of it needs testing and what its test campaigns
recorded, for an environment, over HTTP and MCP. The two sprint reads take the
sprint by name, or the present one, the one whose state is =STARTED=, and
answer inside the caller's view. The two campaign reads answer a run, so a
tester can resume it, and a campaign's notes, so a developer can address
one.

* Notes

- The sprint messages other than the two requests match the structs of
  =compass.db/types/testing.hpp= in name, member names, types, optionality
  and default. =scenario_state= is the enumeration =:values:= on
  =scenario_status::state= names.
- The two requests are one struct in C++, =sprint_request=, which
  =query::sprint_state= and =query::what_needs_testing= both take. A message
  states one route and one tool, so the model splits it into
  =sprint_state_request= and =what_needs_testing_request=, with the same
  members. Generating them changes the two functions' parameter types.
- The campaign reads are new and no query function answers them yet.
  =run_finish= and =note_address= match the structs of
  =compass.db/types/campaign_export.hpp=, which the export already reads
  from the same rows; =step_result= and =campaign_note= are close to
  =exported_step= and =exported_note= but carry what a tester and a
  developer need, a step's body and no screenshots.
- Reading an attachment's bytes back, and serving the images a rendered
  page shows, are not modelled: they answer a file, not JSON, and the
  operation model has no form for that yet. They belong to
  [[id:186C4AF8-CAB4-4754-B8AC-76D571B1B3E9][Serve the documents over HTTP]].
