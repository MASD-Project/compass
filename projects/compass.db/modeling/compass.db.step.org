One row per heading right under =* Steps= of a test scenario, in order: its
title, and its text without the heading line and any =Result= subheading.

* Flags
:PROPERTIES:
:product:    compass
:component:  db
:schema:     test
:profile:    owned-child-row
:END:

The profile =owned-child-row= fixes =read_only=, =current_state= and =insert_conflict: do_nothing=, as =content-addressed-row= does, for a row its owner's key and its =ord= identify, deleted with its owner.

* Columns

** hash
:PROPERTIES:
:type:         bytea
:cpp_type:     store::types::blob_id
:primary_key:  true
:key_kind:     content_hash
:END:

The scenario's key, the blob ID of its document: the owner's key. The writer takes it as an argument, so the row type leaves it out.

** ord
:PROPERTIES:
:type:         integer
:cpp_type:     std::uint32_t
:primary_key:  true
:key_kind:     ord
:END:

The step's place under =* Steps=, from 0: the index of the row in =scenario_row::steps=.

** title
:PROPERTIES:
:type:  text
:END:

The step heading's title.

** body
:PROPERTIES:
:type:  text
:END:

The step's text, without the heading line and any =Result= subheading.

* Foreign keys

** hash
:PROPERTIES:
:references:  test.scenario
:on_delete:   cascade
:END:

Deleting the scenario, which its document's deletion does, deletes its steps.

* C++

The row is =compass::db::types::step_row= of
=compass.db/types/step_row.hpp=, generated: =title= and =body=; =write_copy=
of =compass.db/io/step_copy.hpp= takes the hash and the ord beside it.

* Notes

The table has no index beyond its key.
