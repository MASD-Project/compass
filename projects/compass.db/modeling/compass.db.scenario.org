One row per corpus document of =#+type: test_scenario=, from its =Scenario
Info= table. It shares the document's key and is deleted with it.
=definition= hashes the page without its results, so writing a result into
the page changes the document's hash and leaves the definition's alone.

* Flags
:PROPERTIES:
:product:    compass
:component:  db
:schema:     test
:profile:    content-addressed-row
:END:

The profile =content-addressed-row= fixes =read_only=, =current_state= and =insert_conflict: do_nothing=: a row is keyed by the blob ID of what it describes, is written once with =ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING= and never updated.

The row is a one-to-one extension of its document rather than an owned child
with an =ord=, so it binds =content-addressed-row=, like the document.

* C++

The row is =compass::db::types::scenario_row= of
=compass.db/types/scenario_row.hpp=, generated: the six nullable texts and
=definition=. The steps a scenario owns are no column, so they are in the
hand-written =test_scenario= of =compass.db/types/corpus_document.hpp=, a
=scenario_row= with its =std::vector<step_row> steps=.

* Notes

The key is also a foreign key. The model states it under =* Columns= and
under =* Foreign keys=, and =schema.sql= writes both as the one column
definition migration 1 has, =hash bytea PRIMARY KEY REFERENCES
corpus.document ON DELETE CASCADE=.
