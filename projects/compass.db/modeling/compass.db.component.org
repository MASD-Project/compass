The files of =compass.db= that belong to the component rather than to one of
its tables. The one such facet it switches on is
[[id:7D0FA277-D3F7-48E9-BCDF-7A88592119E3][masd.cpp.codec]], the JSON codec
runtime copied from ORE Studio, which lands in =include/compass.db/codec/=
and =src/codec/= in the namespace =compass::db::codec=. Every row codec of
[[id:48CA6C74-8EF8-45B2-B6D6-CC6B508EE0D8][masd.cpp.json]] beside it, and the
codecs of the service's messages, are written against it.
