This page is the archetype that writes the header of an operation model's
messages for the [[id:6BA0459B-525A-464F-954D-B0E83E83B92D][masd.cpp.protocol]]
facet. It is ORE Studio's =ores.cpp.protocol= header for an operation with
the wire codec on, less MessagePack, NATS and the export macro, and with the
enumerations the fields declare.

* Origin

ORE Studio's
=projects/ores.codegen/library/templates/ores.cpp.protocol.protocol_header.org= at commit
=bc0702ad46b8a6097ce5242ece7ba01067699f60=. The intro above states how the copy differs.

* Context

The context is the library's values and =masd_operation=:

- =namespace=, =protocol_guard=, =codec_namespace= and =codec_root=.
- =includes=: each =include= of the model's =* Includes= block with the
  standard headers the enumerations and the traits need, standard headers
  first, each sorted and named once.
- =has_base64=: whether a field is encoded as base64.
- =enums=: each with =name=, =qualified=, =guard=, =listed= and
  =values=, each a =value= with =last=.
- =messages=: each with =name=, =qualified=, =routed=, =http_method=,
  =http_path=, =has_body= and =fields=, each with =member=, =message=,
  =cpp_type=, =initializer=, =http_in=, =http_name= and =may_be_absent=. A
  routed request lists its fields again by where its route reads them, as
  =path_fields=, =query_fields= and =body_fields=, for the templates that
  bind it.

A field that =may_be_absent= is stated so in =wire_fields=, and its reader
keeps its default when a document leaves it out: a field that is not a path
field and is a vector or a message, has a =:default:= or states =:required:
false=, as the service's binding rules say. A request with a body field also
states =body_fields=, the shape of the body its route reads, and its wire
traits read a body and give its schema.

An enumeration's declaration sits in the model's namespace and its
=text_form= in the codec's, so the guard is opened twice and defined at the
second: the first header to declare it defines both halves, and a later one
skips both.
