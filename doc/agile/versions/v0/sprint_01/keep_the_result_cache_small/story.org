* Goal

The result cache a repository's worktrees share stays a few times the size
of what they use now, however long they run. On 2026-11-20 ORE Studio's
cache was 19.8 GB after three days, 88% of it named by no worktree, and it
filled the SSD: [[id:EDCF07F7-7981-4BF8-B908-8DD4E6D9121F][Why the result cache grows]]. What is the same across
worktrees and runs is stored once, and the cache keeps each worktree's last
two or three runs, not every result ever written.

* Acceptance

- An edit to one file writes that file's scan facts, not every file's.
- Worktrees on the same content share the facts and findings of their
  scans.
- No run leaves the cache holding more than each worktree's last two or
  three results of a unit and what they share.
- ORE Studio's cache, measured as the investigation measured it, after a
  day of normal use across its worktrees.
