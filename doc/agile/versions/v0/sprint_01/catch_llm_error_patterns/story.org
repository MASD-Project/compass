* Goal

The five most frequent and costly error patterns LLMs introduce into ORE Studio are caught by compass checks before a build, each proven on the past commit where it happened.

* Acceptance

- Failures are collected from failed CI runs, fix-up commits after LLM-authored commits, PR review comments and ORE Studio's memories.
- Error messages are normalised and grouped into patterns ranked by frequency and cost.
- A check exists for each of the top five patterns, fires on the commit that introduced the error and stays quiet on the fix.
