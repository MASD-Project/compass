* Result

- Every ORE Studio org file parses with no error. A full parse takes about a
  quarter of a second on 8 threads and two thirds of a second on one, well
  inside the 5 s cold budget, and type resolution adds about 3 ms.
- 204 documents carry no =#+type:=; most are files under =evidence/=
  directories beside tasks.
- 11 types in use are not defined by ORE Studio's own contract: test_scenario
  (165), archetype (161), timeline (134), facet (46), feature (34),
  release_notes (29), profile (14), technical_space (8), dataset_overview (2),
  entity_view (2), analysis (1). They are gaps for ORE Studio to close; the
  registry reports them rather than guessing.
