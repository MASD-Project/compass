* Goal

compass regenerates all of ORE Studio's generated code byte-for-byte identical to ores.codegen, in less than 30 seconds for a full run, touching only files whose content changes.

* Acceptance

- Every ORE Studio component regenerates byte-for-byte identical to ores.codegen at the same commit.
- A full regeneration of ORE Studio takes less than 30 seconds on 8 cores, measured against the baseline.
- clang-format and prettier run only for outputs whose raw render changed, through a cache keyed by the raw output hash and formatter version.
- Templates are tangled from their org sources by compass, with no Emacs process.
