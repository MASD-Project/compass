* Goal

Every environment's org documents are in compass's database, each stored once
by the blob ID of its content, and stay in step with the files without anyone
asking: after an agent's edit, at the end of each compass run, after a pull,
a branch switch or a rebase, and at a deploy. A row no environment and no test
run names is collected.

* Acceptance

- The corpus tables =document=, =heading=, =section=, =link=,
  =environment= and =placement= are filled from compass's parse as the
  store holds it; no second parser.
- Each section's =tsvector= is computed in the insert: the document's title
  weighs A, the heading path B and the text D, under the database's text
  search configuration.
- Pages of =#+type: test_scenario= fill =test.scenario= and =test.step=: the
  task and story from =Scenario Info=, the target, the environment, the
  steps under =* Steps= without their =Result=, and a definition hash that
  a written result does not change.
- Inserts use =ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING=, and the documents and the placement
  swap are written in one transaction.
- =compass sync [--environment <name>] [--commit <rev>]= syncs a worktree, or
  a release from git's objects.
- =generate= and =check= of a worktree sync at their end when a database is
  configured; without one they behave exactly as before.
- The edit hook syncs the one file it names.
- =compass db hooks install= writes git's =post-checkout=, =post-merge= and
  =post-rewrite= hooks, and leaves a hook that is not compass's as it is,
  saying so.
- An environment is named by =#+environment:= of the local settings, else by
  the worktree's directory.
- Collection removes the documents no placement and no =activity.run= names,
  as part of a sync once the corpus has doubled, and on =compass db prune=.
- Measured on a copy of ORE Studio: the counts, the first, unchanged and
  one-file sync times, the size, two environments sharing rows, and
  collection after one is dropped.

* Plan

- Reuse the pipeline's steps: =list=, =open_files=, =resolve= and =load= of
  =compass.cli= give each document's blob ID, its bytes and its tables from
  the store. A sync resolves every document, asks the database which IDs it
  lacks, and loads only those; =db::ops::extract= turns their tables into
  rows on every thread. Documents it had to parse go into the store too.
- One transaction per sync: upsert the environment row (which serialises
  syncs of one environment), stage the placements and the rows with =COPY=,
  lock every placed document =FOR KEY SHARE=, insert with =ON CONFLICT DO
  NOTHING=, then delete, update and add placements. A placement whose
  document a collection removed since the sync looked makes the transaction
  roll back and the sync load it and try again, up to three times.
- Collection deletes =FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED=, so it never removes a document
  a sync is placing, and takes an advisory lock it does not wait for.
- The edit hook's sync hashes the one file, places it alone, and removes its
  placement when the file is gone.
- A git hook runs =compass-ng sync= in the background, so a pull is not
  slowed by it.

* Notes

- The numbers were measured on a shared machine at load average 10 to 16 on 8
  cores, so they are indicative. The first sync is bound by the server
  computing each section's =tsvector= and maintaining the GIN index: the
  section insert alone took 10.7 s of a 15.8 s write. A first sync happens
  once per database; every later one loads only new content.
- A no-change sync is bound by listing the worktree's files with =git
  ls-files= (208 ms of 390 ms); the database answers in under 10 ms.
- The two files of ORE Studio with the same bytes share one document row,
  so 11,182 paths place 11,180 documents.
- =test.scenario.definition= also leaves out a top-level =* Results=
  heading, which ORE Studio's scenarios use for the outcome of the whole
  scenario; it is a result, like the =Result= of a step.
- Anchors follow the design: a heading's =:ID:=, else a slug of its path made
  unique in the document. Render documents as HTML computes anchors too, and
  the two must agree; =db::ops::slug= is the one to share.
