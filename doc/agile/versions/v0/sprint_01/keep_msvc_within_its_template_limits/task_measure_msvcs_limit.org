* Acceptance

- For each mechanism the investigation finds, the threshold MSVC fails at is measured, and whether a unit's instantiations accumulate is answered by a probe.

* Plan

Probes are generated translation units compiled with MSVC by the
=MSVC probes= workflow (=.github/workflows/msvc-probes.yml=, run by
hand), with the flags of ORE Studio's release build.
=build/probes/msvc_c1202/probe.py= generates and compiles them and
writes each one's outcome: ok, C1202, or another error.

Mechanism B, a unit's accumulated context, first:

1. Do the column lists alone reproduce unit 55? Synthetic entities with
   ORE Studio's =columns.hpp= (a copy, at =e866b76f3e=), sized and
   ordered as units 53 and 55 include them. If =unit55= fails and
   =unit53= compiles, the column lists carry the cost; if neither fails,
   the unit's other templates do, and the next probe is unit 55 itself.
2. Width against count. One entity of growing width, and many entities
   of one width, to see whether the cost is per chain or per unit, and
   to bracket the budget.
3. Bisect the bracket to a threshold the check can use.

Mechanism A, one reflected type, next: reflect-cpp at ORE Studio's
version, one struct by members, nesting and the alternatives of a
tagged variant.
