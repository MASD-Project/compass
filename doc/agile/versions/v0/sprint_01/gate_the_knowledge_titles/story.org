* Goal

ORE Studio's house style says a knowledge page's title is a noun phrase,
like the title of an academic paper: never a sentence, with no finite verb.
The claim the page argues goes in the first sentence of its summary. About
76 old pages still carry sentence titles, and agents copy them into new
pages. No gate stops them.

This story gives compass a check codec, =knowledge_titles=, that reports a
knowledge page whose title is a sentence, and one whose file name is not
its title in snake case. compass holds the engine and the project holds the
data, as for the other checks. The verbs, the words that are verbs only
after a determiner, the determiners, the paths to skip and the settings are
tables of a check document whose =:CODEC:= names the codec. The old pages
pass through =compass check --ratchet= until they are retitled, so the
check carries no exception rows.

* Acceptance

- compass's code names no verb, no ORE page and no ORE path. The words,
  the skipped paths, the structure-note tag and the pointer to the house
  style are rows of the project's check document.
- A title holding a listed finite verb is a =sentence_title= finding. A
  listed ambiguous word, a verb that is also a plural noun, counts only
  when the title opens with a listed determiner.
- A structure note is not held to the verbs, and its file name ends in the
  hub suffix. A file name that is not the title in snake case is a
  =title_file_mismatch= finding, and a setting turns that rule off.
- On a copy of ORE Studio's tree with the draft check document, the
  findings are counted and sampled, and their false positives listed.

* Decisions

- 2026-11-22: questions and /how/, /why/ and /which/ titles are not
  exempt. They are flagged when they hold a finite verb. (Product owner.)
- 2026-11-22: the prototypes are out of scope. ORE Studio lists
  =doc/prototypes/= in the check document's =Skip= table. (Product owner.)
- 2026-11-22: old pages pass through the ratchet only. The codec has no
  exception mechanism of its own, and the check document no exception
  rows. (Product owner.)
- 2026-11-22: the file-name rule is a separate rule that a setting turns
  off, in case it proves noisy. (Product owner.)
- 2026-11-22: compass's own =.compass.org= leaves the codec off. compass
  has no check document for it, and most of compass's knowledge pages have
  file names that are not their titles in snake case.

* Out of scope

- Retitling ORE Studio's old pages. That is ORE Studio's work, and the
  ratchet lets them stand until it is done.
- Parsing English. The check is a word list. A sentence whose verb is in
  its base form after a plural noun passes, and the check document says so.
