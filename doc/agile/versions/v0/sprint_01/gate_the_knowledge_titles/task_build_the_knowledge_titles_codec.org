* Goal

compass has a check codec, =knowledge_titles=, that reports a knowledge page
whose title is a sentence and one whose file name is not its title in snake
case, from the words, paths and settings of a project's check document. ORE
Studio has a draft of that document, proved on a copy of its tree.

* Acceptance

- The codec reads five tables of the check document: =Finite verbs=,
  =Ambiguous=, =Determiners=, =Skip= and =Settings=. A missing document,
  table or column, or a wrong row, is one =knowledge_titles/unreadable=
  finding.
- =sentence_title=: a title holding a word of =Finite verbs=, or a word of
  =Ambiguous= when its first word is in =Determiners=. Words are the title
  lowercased and split on every character that is not a letter or an
  apostrophe.
- =title_file_mismatch=: a file name that is not the title in snake case,
  or a structure note's that does not end in the hub suffix. The setting
  =title_file_mismatch= set to =no= turns it off.
- Catch2 tests fail on each of these defects: a sentence title passing, a
  noun phrase flagged, an ambiguous word counted without a determiner, a
  structure note held to the verbs, a skipped path read, and a wrong snake
  case.

* Plan

- A corpus codec in =compass.docs=, modelled on =skill_levels=: the check
  document's tables are its =data= option, read through =tables_for=.
- It reads every document and keeps those whose =#+type:= keyword is
  =knowledge=, so it works on a corpus whose types are not registered.
- A finding sits on the page's =#+title:= line and its message holds the
  title and the verbs, never a count or a line, so =check --ratchet= tells
  an old page from a new one.

* Notes

- The codec's 102 =sentence_title= findings are the titles a Python model
  of the same rule, with the same word lists, flags: the two sets are
  identical.
- One false positive remains among the 102: /The workflow instance and step
  states/, where /states/ is a plural noun after /The/. Titles with a verb
  in a relative clause (/The identity an adapter runs as/) and question
  titles are flagged by decision.
- Known misses: a sentence whose subject is a plural noun and whose verb is
  in its base form and also a noun (/Short-date swap points run between
  their own two value dates/, /Public publishers publish daily fixings/).
- compass's own =.compass.org= leaves the codec off: compass has no check
  document for it, and 30 of its knowledge pages have file names that are
  not their titles in snake case.
