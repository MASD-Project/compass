* Acceptance

- Rules from Luhmann, Ahrens, Matuschak and zettelkasten.de that our zettelkasten page lacks are recorded there.
- Words, sections, inbound and outbound links are measured per document type over the ORE Studio corpus.
- Gates cover orphans, dead ends, missing back-links, size, bare See also links, see-also-only pages, structure note shape, aside sections and broken links, with ratchet mode.
- Reports cover clusters without a structure note, untagged hubs, islands, load-bearing pages, possible duplicates and pages with no link outside their cluster.
