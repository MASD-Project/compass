* Result

24 of the 28 org and cross-artefact gates are compass check codecs, each
matching its original on ORE Studio as it is and with planted violations. Two
new components hold them: =compass.docs= (8 gates over org documents and plain
files; =org_links= and =heading_numbers= run per document) and
=compass.crosscheck= (16 gates over models with C++, SQL, templates or
generated output).

Baselines moved to inline exceptions: entity table writes (136), money
decimal columns (7) and template sections (8); the empty subject-conformance
baseline and the model-drift known list are gone. That is why a check of ORE
as it is now reports 226 findings: 24 drift files and 202 baseline entries
that ORE would carry as exceptions.

Not ported:

- The two =--self-test= gates prove their scripts fail on planted defects;
  the codecs' Catch2 tests plant the same defects on every build.
- no-committed-credentials reads =.env=, which is git-ignored, so a codec
  would never see it.
- conventions-coverage regenerates a dataset from ORE's XML samples; it moves
  to the generator gates.

Decisions to revisit:

- The warm check measured 9.2-10.3 s at load 32, against the story's 10 s
  budget. It needs a measurement on a quiet machine, and the dependency
  record's read (about 0.9 s) is the known saving.
- Gates that apply a regex to org text keep the regex instead of the parse,
  because the parse differs on indented or upper-case blocks.
- =compass.docs= and =compass.crosscheck= depend on =compass.cpp= for its
  Python-emulation helpers; those belong in =compass.platform=.
