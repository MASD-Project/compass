* Acceptance

- All ported gates over the whole ORE Studio tree run in under 10 seconds warm.

* Plan

- The gates, from the [[id:AFF665C4-739C-4BDB-93BD-9D547351AE4F][gate classification]], plus parameter-value-sets-only and
  conventions-coverage moved here by the earlier ports: each regenerates an
  artefact and compares it with the committed file.
- Port each generator as a codec that emits its artefact, so =compass
  check='s drift findings replace the gate and =compass generate= replaces
  the script. Tangling reuses the =tangle= codec, which already matches Emacs
  on the templates.
- Check each output byte-identical to the original generator's output on ORE
  Studio 545cafca45, as it is and after a planted source change, and explain
  any difference from the committed file.
- Measure the warm =compass check= with every ported gate on a quiet machine
  against the story's 10 s budget; if it is over, cut the dependency record's
  read cost.

* Result

24 of the 31 generator gates are codecs: their artefacts are compass outputs
byte-identical to the original generators on ORE Studio 545cafca45, so
=compass check= reports their drift and =compass generate= replaces the
scripts. The warm check with every ported gate is inside the story's 10 s
budget.

Ported: tangle-shell, tangle-http and tangle-templates (the tangle codecs with
a directory option), cmake-sources, skill-catalogue, skill-type-tables,
skill-recipes, structure-notes, skill-levels, recipe-inventories,
backlog-indexes, physical-inventory, pending-models, trade-type-coverage,
ore-renderings-tables, script-library-seed, curve-sections-wiring,
curve-column-guards, convention-kind-terms, route-inventory and
ore-output-catalogue.

Real drift found in ORE Studio at 545cafca45: 9 shell recipe scripts never
re-tangled after their recipes gained an argument, a capture missing from the
backlog inbox index, and 19 stale line numbers in the route inventory.

The rest move to [[id:555CA760-38D2-40B5-97C5-8ED858EAF3BB][Port the large generator gates]]: conventions-dataset,
ore-parameters-seed, file-types-seed, conventions-coverage, xml-bindings-drift,
er-diagram, ore-element-map, ore-parameter-value-sets,
parameter-value-sets-only and lint (a diagnostics check, not a generator).
The value-set gates stop on ORE's =r.id= defect; with it fixed they pass, which
defines their verdict.

Decisions to revisit:

- An output may have only one producer, so skill-levels split into an
  inventory and a check; letting several codecs edit one page needs a change
  to the output rules.
- Where a script reports a problem and writes nothing, the codec writes
  nothing, so =compass check= is silent where the gate fails (convention-kind-
  terms on =r.id=).
- The checkout listing now leaves out files deleted from the worktree.
- Tables are read from ORE's Python modules through the literal reader where
  possible; earlier ports copied theirs into the codecs.
- XML is read through expat, with ElementTree's text and tail rules.
