- Each of the 25 gates gives its original's verdict on the same ORE Studio commit, as it is and with planted violations.
- The C++ and SQL gates together add under 2 seconds to a warm =compass check= of ORE Studio.

    unique-type-names, forward-declarable-includes, handler-registrars,

    xml-family-census, shell-command-units, shell-menus, core-link-edges,
    event-registrars, history-registrars, shell-command-composition,

    rls-widenings, parameter-value-sets-only, curve-parameter-paths.
- Port each one's exact rules into =compass.cpp= or =compass.sql=, reusing
  the scanners the slowest gates added, in order of cost.
- Check each against its original on ORE Studio 545cafca45, as it is and
  with planted violations. Baselines become inline exceptions where they are
  line- or file-shaped.
