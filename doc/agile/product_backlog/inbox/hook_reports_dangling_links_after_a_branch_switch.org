* What

Reported by ORE Studio's session on 2026-11-19, with compass-ng
=79af4ff= installed. After =git switch -c= from =origin/main=, the
PostToolUse hook reported six =lint/dangling_link= findings on one edit.
One pointed at =8AD9ABB7=, the :ID: of a capture the session had just
written with the Write tool, not Edit; the others were in sprint_30
stories. A full =compass-ng check= straight after reported none of them.
Reproduce it: switch branches, create a document with Write, link to it
from another, edit that one, and compare the hook with a full check.

* Why

A hook that reports what the full check does not costs the agent a
detour on every edit after a switch, and teaches it to ignore the hook.
The guess, not checked: the hook reads the listing record of the
checkout, which the switch or the new file left stale.
