This is the product identity of compass-ng — the System 5 document in our
cybernetic information architecture, where identity is the durable policy layer.
It states what compass-ng is, who it serves, and what it will not do.

* Vision

** Vision statement

compass-ng is a side-kick for LLMs working on a software project. It holds the
project's documentation, knowledge and work as one linked corpus of documents,
and lets LLMs and humans query and change that corpus quickly. The work of the
LLMs is itself managed by compass-ng, as documents.

compass-ng ports the document management of compass — compass-ng's
=projects/compass.legacy= and the =doc/= contract it serves — to a C++
data-oriented design. "compass-ng" names the new implementation; "compass"
names the old one.

** Qualities

- *Fast.* Queries answer in milliseconds on a corpus the size of compass-ng's
  (about 10,000 documents) and scale well beyond it.
- *Memory-efficient.* Data-oriented layout; no per-document object graphs.
- *Concurrent.* At least 10 clients at once, mostly reads with a steady stream
  of writes.
- *Self-hosting.* compass-ng manages its own documentation and work as soon as
  it can.

* Audience

| Client | Frontend                       | Needs                                                                         |
|--------+--------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| LLMs   | =compass= CLI, binary protocol | Fast, compact, unambiguous answers: find, show, link, create and update work. |
| Humans | Website                        | Browse everything as a wiki.                                                  |
| Humans | ImGui viewer                   | See the rich data in real time, in particular the knowledge graph.            |

* Out of scope

Everything in compass-ng's compass that is not document management:
environments, deployment, systemd, NATS, builds and pull requests.
